# Prompts from the Video

These are the exact two prompts I used in the video. Just paste them into Claude
(with the skills installed) and swap in your own character, count, or vibe.

---

## 1. Instagram content — `ai-instagram-engine`

```
Give me 10 Instagram posts of my character, with baddie vibes.
It has to look like a real Instagram feed post, not a photoshoot.
Use Nano Banana Pro, 4K resolution and a 3:4 aspect ratio.
Show me your plan and the total price first, and don't start before I say go.
Make sure to use the ai-instagram-engine skill.
```

Swap freely: the number (`10`), the vibe (`baddie`, `soft-girl`, `luxury`,
`fitness`, `egirl`), or the aspect ratio (`9:16` for stories).

---

## 2. Full character dataset — `ai-dataset-builder`

```
I have a new character — only 3 pictures of her in the anchors folder.
Build me the complete dataset: face portraits, the emotion grid and the full 360.
Use Nano Banana Pro, 4K resolution and a 3:4 aspect ratio.
Show me the plan and the cost first.
Make sure to use the ai-dataset-builder skill.
```

This is your foundation — from here you can train a LoRA on any model you want.

---

**Tip:** always let Claude show you the plan and the price first, then say "go".
You only ever pay for the images you actually generate.
