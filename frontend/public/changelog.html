Changelog

October 20, 2026

Sign up for Buyerly

Anyone can now create a Buyerly account. There's no invite list anymore: go to buyerly.app/signup, enter your email, and set up your workspace in a minute.

New sign-up screen

Sign up with your email, confirm with a one-time code, and you're in. Already have an account? Log in from the same screen.

Sign-in emails

Login codes arrive in a clean, readable email, with the code easy to copy.

Improvements
  • A new homepage at buyerly.app, in light and dark.
  • Sort Settings lists (Members, Teams, Labels) by any column header.
October 17, 2026

Invite your team

Bring your team in while you set up the workspace. Share the workspace's invite link, or invite people by email, each with a role: Workspace admin, Buyer or Viewer.

Invites from Settings → Members

Choose the role for everyone you invite. Mistyped addresses are flagged before anything is sent.

Improvements
  • Settings have a cleaner layout in light and dark.
  • Refined sidebar, tabs, tooltips and header buttons.
October 14, 2026

Rules board and filters

Filter, group and sort your rules, and see them as a board. Filter by status, label, action, level, metric, window or ad account, and group the board by any of them.

A new New rule window

A rule now reads like a sentence: when an ad set has these conditions over this window, then do this. Leave the name empty and Buyerly names the rule after what it does.

Ads Manager columns and periods

Show any metric as a column — from Spend and CTR to Link clicks, Leads, CPL and Purchases — then filter and sort by it, group by campaign, and pick the period.

October 13, 2026

Ad accounts page

All your ad accounts on one page, grouped by team. Open it from the sidebar or with G then K, and open any account for its details.

Ready-made rules

The rule examples now follow a launch ladder for new campaigns, and rules can use Link clicks.

October 11, 2026

Labels for rules

Rule groups are now labels. A rule can have several labels, each with a name, a color and a description. Create one right from the label picker, or manage them all in Settings → Rules → Labels.

Lifetime window

Rules can check spend and results since launch, not only today or the last few days.

Your view is saved

Filters and display options work the same on every list and are kept for each member, across reloads and devices.

Recently deleted

Deleted rules have their own page, where you can bring them back.

October 10, 2026

Teams

Teams group the people in your workspace with the ad accounts they work on. Create, rename and retire teams in Settings → Teams.

Settings, rebuilt

New Profile (with job title and username), Security & access, and Members pages.

Details pane

Open a details pane on the right side of Ads Manager and Rules.

October 7, 2026

Several accounts in one browser

Stay signed in to more than one account and switch between their workspaces without logging out. Use Add an account… from the workspace menu.

Buyerly on your phone

Rules, Ads Manager and Inbox fit small screens.

Member roles

Change a member's role, remove a member, or leave a workspace.

Improvements
  • An open tab tells you when a newer version is out and offers to reload.
  • Security & access shows the city each session signed in from.
October 6, 2026

Rules in Ads Manager

Attach rules to campaigns and ad sets right from Ads Manager, on your phone too. Select rows and choose Apply rule….

Status on campaigns

See where a rule stands on each campaign: when it last checked, what it last did, and why it's quiet.

Which rule wins

When several rules match the same campaign, Buyerly shows which one acts first.
