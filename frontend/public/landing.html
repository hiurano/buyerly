Features
Tech specs

Ad accounts

Every connected Meta ad account on one page, grouped by team, with its status, today’s spend, attached rules and whether automation is on.

Tech specs

Metrics and columns

Any metric as a column on campaigns, ad sets and ads: spend, impressions, CTR, CPC, link clicks, leads, CPL, registrations, purchases and more.

Tech specs

Filters and sorting

Filter and sort by any metric, status or attached rule. Filters and display options stay as you left them after a reload.

Tech specs

Grouping and periods

Group ad sets and ads by campaign, and switch the period between today, yesterday and the last 3 or 7 days.

Rules
and automations

Rules read like a sentence: when an ad set spends $20 with no leads, turn it off. They check every few minutes, around the clock, and act or notify you, with Undo.

Features
Tech specs

Turn off and on

Rules turn campaigns, ad sets and ads off, or back on, when their conditions are met. Every action can be undone from the Inbox.

Tech specs

Budget up and down

Raise or lower an ad set’s budget by a percentage when it does well or badly, with a daily cap you set.

Tech specs

Ready-made examples

Start from tested examples, like stopping an ad set that spends without leads, and adjust the numbers to your account.

Tech specs

Board view

See rules as a list or a board, grouped by status or label, and filter them by action, metric, window or ad account.

Features
Tech specs

Roles

Workspace admins manage members, invites and settings. Buyers work with accounts, campaigns and rules. Viewers see the workspace without changing it.

Tech specs

Teams

Split the workspace into teams, for example one per vertical or geo, and group ad accounts by team.

Tech specs

Invite link

Share one invite link, or invite people by email with a role. Admins can reset the link or turn it off at any time.

Tech specs

Workspaces

Belong to several workspaces and switch between them, or stay signed in to more than one account at once.
