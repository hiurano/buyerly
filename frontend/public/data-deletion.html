Data deletion

Effective date: September 20, 2026

How to stop connected access and request deletion of your Buyerly data.

  1. Send a deletion request
  2. Stop connected access
  3. What happens next

1. Send a deletion request

Email contact@buyerly.app from your Buyerly account email with the subject Buyerly data deletion request. Identify your workspace and say whether you want to delete a Meta connection or your Buyerly account. If you no longer have access to that email, contact us to arrange ownership verification.

We may ask for information needed to verify your authority over the account or workspace. Never send passwords, access tokens, cookies or sign-in codes.

2. Stop connected access

You can disconnect a Meta connection in Buyerly or remove Buyerly in Meta’s Business Integrations or Apps and Websites settings. Disconnecting in Buyerly deletes the stored connection token and disables linked advertising accounts in Buyerly. Revoking permission in Meta stops access under that authorisation.

Neither step by itself erases all historical records held by Buyerly. Send a deletion request for those records. This process does not delete campaigns or accounts on Meta.

3. What happens next

After verification, we delete the personal data covered by your request, including connection credentials and profile data as applicable. We assess related advertising data, rules and shared workspace records according to your authority and the rights of other members.

We explain any limited retention needed for security, legal obligations or disputes. Backup copies expire through the backup-retention cycle. We respond within applicable legal time limits and confirm the outcome. More information is in Privacy.
